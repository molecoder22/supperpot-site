Privacy Policy

Supperpot · last updated 23 September 2026

Supperpot is a recipe app for one-pot meals. It has no accounts and never asks you to sign in. This page explains what the app does with data, in plain words.

What stays on your phone

Your kitchen setup (cookware, household size, diet, priorities), saved recipes, weekly plan, grocery list, notes, ratings, cooking history and the basics you tick are stored only on your device. We do not upload them and cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also clear them any time in Settings → "Clear all my data".

Recipe generation

When you ask for a new recipe, the app sends your chosen filters (cookware, time, protein, diet tags, servings, priorities) and, if you typed one, the list of what you have to our server, which asks an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) to write the recipe. No name, email, phone number, location or advertising identifier is sent. The server keeps a random device identifier created by the app, the date and a count of recipes made, so it can apply the daily allowance. That identifier cannot be linked to you.

Snap my groceries

If you use the camera or choose a photo, the picture is sent to the same server and passed to the AI model to list the food it sees. The photo is processed and discarded; it is not stored on our server and is not used to train any model. Camera and photo access are used only when you tap the button.

Recipe illustrations

New recipes get an illustration generated from the recipe text by an image model (OpenAI's GPT Image, through fal.ai). Only the recipe's title and ingredient names are sent.

Supperpot Plus

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. We receive from Apple only whether a subscription is active and when it expires, never your payment details. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

Notifications

The daily "Tonight's pick" reminder and cooking timers are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Analytics

The app keeps simple anonymous counts of feature use (for example how many recipes were cooked) on your device. No analytics or advertising SDK is included. Our server logs may contain the random device identifier and request timestamps for reliability and abuse prevention; they are deleted within 30 days.

Children

Supperpot is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Your choices

Everything above is optional: the recipe library, cook mode and the plan work without sending anything anywhere. Turning off camera or photo access in iOS Settings disables Snap my groceries only.
