Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 September 2026

Supperpot is a recipe app for one-pot meals. Signing in is optional: you can use the whole app without an account. This page explains, in plain words, what the app does with data.

The short version. Your recipes, plan, list and notes stay on your phone. When you ask for a new recipe or scan a photo, that request goes to our server and on to an AI model, tied only to a random number, and the photo is thrown away straight after. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google we also hold your email address, and nothing more. Nothing is sold, nothing is used for advertising, and there are no trackers.

Signing in is optional

You can tap "Continue without an account" and use every part of the app. Nothing is withheld, and we never ask again except from the Settings screen.

If you do sign in, with Apple or with Google, we receive from them two things: an account identifier and your email address. We do not receive your password, your contacts, your photos, your Google or Apple account contents, or anything else. With Sign in with Apple you may choose "Hide My Email", in which case we only ever see Apple's forwarding address and never your real one.

We use that email address for exactly one purpose: to recognise you when you open Supperpot on another phone, so your free trial, your daily recipe count and your Supperpot Plus subscription follow you instead of starting over. We do not send marketing email. We do not sell, rent or share the address, and there is no third party with access to it besides the hosting provider that runs our database.

Signing out on the phone removes the account from it immediately. To delete the account and its email address from our server entirely, email us and we will remove it.

What stays on your phone

Your kitchen setup (cookware, household size, diet, priorities), saved recipes, weekly plan, grocery list, notes, ratings, cooking history and the basics you tick are stored only on your device. We do not upload them and cannot see them, whether or not you are signed in. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also clear them any time in Settings, "Clear all my data".

Writing a new recipe

When you ask for a new recipe, the app sends your chosen filters (cookware, time, protein, diet tags, servings, priorities) and, if you typed one, the list of what you have, to our server, which asks an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) to write the recipe. No name, email, phone number, location or advertising identifier is sent.

The server stores a random identifier created by the app on first launch, the date, and how many recipes you have made that day, so it can apply the daily allowance. If you are not signed in, that identifier is not linked to you, your Apple ID or your device's own identifiers, and we cannot use it to find out who you are. If you are signed in, the count is held against your account instead, so it is the same on every phone you use.

Snap my groceries

If you take a photo or choose one, the picture is sent to the same server and passed to the AI model, which lists the food it can see. The photo is processed and then discarded. It is not stored on our server, not shown to anyone, and not used to train any model. Camera and photo access are only used when you tap the button.

Recipe illustrations

New recipes get an illustration generated from the recipe text by an image model (OpenAI's GPT Image, reached through fal.ai). Only the recipe's title and its ingredient names are sent.

Supperpot Plus

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. We receive from Apple only whether a subscription is active and when it expires. We never see your name, your payment details or your Apple ID. Manage or cancel a subscription in your iPhone settings.

Notifications

The daily pick reminder and the cooking timers are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server and no push service is used.

Analytics and advertising

Supperpot contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no tracking of any kind. It keeps simple anonymous counts of feature use on your device only, which never leave the phone. Our server keeps ordinary request logs containing the random identifier and a timestamp, for reliability and to stop abuse; they are deleted within 30 days.

Children

Supperpot is not directed at children under 13. The only personal data it can collect is an email address, and only from someone who chooses to sign in.

Your choices

  • You never have to sign in. "Continue without an account" gives you the whole app.
  • The 100-recipe library, cook mode, the planner and the grocery list work with no network at all.
  • Turning off camera or photo access in iOS Settings disables Snap my groceries and nothing else.
  • "Clear all my data" in Settings erases everything the app has stored on your phone.
  • Signing out in Settings removes your account from that phone straight away.
  • To have the server delete your account, your email address and your usage record, email us and say so. We will remove all of it.
