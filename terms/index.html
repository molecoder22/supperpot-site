What Supperpot is

Supperpot suggests recipes, writes new ones with an AI model, and guides you through cooking them. It is a cooking aid, not professional advice.

Cook safely. Recipes include cooking times and safe internal temperatures, but ovens, pans and ingredients differ. Use your own judgement, a thermometer for meat, and normal kitchen care. Supperpot does not know about your allergies or medical needs: always read the ingredients yourself before cooking or serving. Recipes written by the AI model are generated automatically and are not reviewed by a person before you see them.
