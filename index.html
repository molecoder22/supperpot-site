How it works

Three taps from a full fridge to food on the table.

1. Show it what you have

Photograph your shelf, your fridge or your shopping. Supperpot lists the food it recognises, and you can fix the list by hand. Or just type it.

2. Get one recipe, not fifty

It writes a single meal built around your ingredients, for the one pot you own, inside the time you have. No scrolling through results.

3. Cook it step by step

Groceries, then prep, then the pot. One step per screen with a short animation of the move, the amounts for that step, a timer and a cue for when it's done.

What it looks like

Hand-illustrated, never stock photos. Swipe sideways.

What's in it

100 illustrated meals

Your cookware only

Plan seven meals and get one grocery list, grouped by supermarket aisle with duplicates merged.
