Supperpot

Snap a photo of your groceries or type what's in the fridge. Supperpot writes a one-pot meal for the cookware you actually own and walks you through it, one step at a time, with a little animation for every move.

Coming to the App Store

What it does

  • Snap my groceries. A photo of your shelf becomes an ingredient list, then a recipe.
  • Only your cookware. Skillet, pot, sheet pan, Instant Pot, slow cooker or rice cooker. Nothing you don't own is ever suggested.
  • Cook mode. Groceries → prep → pot. One step per screen, timers, "done when" cues, amounts per step.
  • 100 illustrated recipes plus new ones written for you. Weekly plan, aisle-grouped grocery list, US or metric.
